Source-side facts (verified against NavigationMenu.tsx 2026-05-11):
- `<nav aria-label={ariaLabel}>` containing `<ul>` of items (line 32-44). Forwards ref to the `<nav>`.
- Leaf NavItem (no children) renders as `<a href={item.href} class="...">` (line 168-174)
- NavItem with children renders as `<button type="button" aria-expanded={isOpen} aria-haspopup="true" aria-controls={dropdownId} onClick={toggle} onKeyDown={handleTriggerKeyDown}>` followed by a sibling `<ul id={dropdownId}>` containing `<a>` children (lines 119-165)
- Dropdown items are plain `<a>` — NOT `role="menuitem"` (Disclosure pattern, NOT Menubar)
- Caret SVG carries `aria-hidden="true"` (line 137)
- handleTriggerKeyDown (line 82-92): only handles ArrowDown — opens dropdown and `requestAnimationFrame(() => firstLink?.focus())`. Pitfall 5 — use waitFor.
- handleKeyDown on the <li> (line 73-80): only handles Escape — closes dropdown, focuses trigger, calls stopPropagation.
- NO ArrowLeft/Right/Up/Home/End handling at any level (gap — Menubar features).
- hover/focus on the <li> opens the dropdown (lines 110-115) — focus-parity with hover; this means TAB-bing through can ALSO open dropdowns. Document but do not test exhaustively.
- onClick on trigger toggles isOpen (line 122). Enter and Space on a native <button> both fire click — paired-keyboard D-02a satisfied implicitly.
- Click-outside closes the dropdown (line 60-71 useEffect).
- The dropdown <ul> uses `visible` / `invisible` class toggles, not conditional render — the <ul> is always in the DOM but ARIA-hidden via visibility when closed. This means `screen.getByRole('list')` will find it regardless of state; assert via aria-expanded on the trigger, not via the <ul> existence.
