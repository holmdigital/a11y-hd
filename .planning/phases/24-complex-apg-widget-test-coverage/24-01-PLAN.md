Source-side facts that constrain test assertions:
- `<input role="combobox" aria-autocomplete="list" aria-expanded={isOpen} aria-haspopup="listbox" aria-controls={listboxId} aria-activedescendant={focusedIndex>=0 ? `${id}-option-${focusedIndex}` : undefined}>`

- Source does NOT render `<LiveRegion>` — no aria-live anywhere except the error `role="alert"`.
